RBI cuts repo rate by 25 bps to stimulate borrowing

In plain terms: borrowing just got cheaper. The RBI — India's central bank — trimmed the rate it charges commercial banks, hoping people and businesses spend and invest more.

Why it matters
Loans get cheaper — home & auto EMIs are set to fall over the coming quarter.
Who benefits
Homebuyers, existing borrowers, and corporations issuing new debt expansion.
Market impact
Sensex and Nifty rally led by banking stocks; bond yields drop sharply.
What happens next
Commercial banks will pass on rate drops to consumer lending within 2–4 weeks.
Key terms, explained
Repo rate — the rate the RBI lends to commercial banks at.
Monetary policy — the tools a central bank uses to manage growth and inflation.
